Business Brain

Teach your AI your business in 10 minutes. Free guide, version 2 (draft for testing).

Works with the AI app you already use: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok. Use your phone or your laptop. Set it up on one, and it shows up on the other.

What is this?

Right now, your AI is like a smart new helper on day one. It doesn't know your business, so it guesses.

The Business Brain fixes that. It's one short note about your business: what you sell, your prices, your customers, and how you talk. You save it inside your AI app once. After that, every answer knows your business.

The 4 steps

  1. Make. Make a project called "My Business." A project is a folder inside your AI app that remembers things.
  2. Talk. Inside that project, your AI asks you easy questions. You answer by typing or talking.
  3. Save. Your AI writes your Business Brain. You save it in the project, so it remembers it forever.
  4. Use. You ask your AI to do real work, like replying to a customer. It already knows your business.

1Make

Make an empty project now. You'll fill it in Step 3. Pick your app:

Which AI do you use?

Claude

  • Phone (Claude app): Menu → Projects → New project. Name it "My Business."
  • Laptop (claude.ai or the Claude desktop app): Projects → New project. Name it "My Business" and create it.

ChatGPT

  • Phone (ChatGPT app): Menu → Projects → the + button. Name it "My Business."
  • Laptop (chatgpt.com or the ChatGPT desktop app): Projects → New project. Name it "My Business."

Gemini

  • Phone (Gemini app): Menu → New notebook. Name it "My Business."
  • Laptop (gemini.google.com): Notebooks → New notebook. Name it "My Business."
  • Don't add a source yet. That comes in Step 3.

Grok

  • Phone (Grok app): Menu → Projects → the + button. Name it "My Business."
  • Laptop (grok.com): Projects → New Project. Name it "My Business."
  • Leave Project Instructions empty for now, and tap ✓ (or Create).
Your project lives in your account, so it shows up on your phone and your laptop. Set it up on one, use it on both.

2Talk

Open your My Business project and start a new chat inside it (in Claude, the button says New session; in ChatGPT, type in the box at the bottom of the Chats tab). Copy the message below, paste it in the chat box, and send it. Then just answer the questions.

Paste it in the chat, where you'd normally type. Not in Instructions, and not as a source. That's Step 3.
You are going to build my Business Brain: one short note about my business that I'll save and reuse.

Interview me like a friendly assistant on their first day:
- Ask ONE question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next one.
- Use simple words. Give a quick example with each question so I know what you mean.
- Change your questions to fit my kind of business.
- If I say "skip", move on.
- Ask 12 questions at most.

Cover these:
1. What my business is called and what I sell
2. Who usually buys from me
3. My prices and main offers
4. How I talk to customers (ask me for 3 words, and a real message I've sent)
5. What makes me different from others like me
6. The questions customers ask me most, and my answers
7. Things I never want you to do or say
8. My main goal this month

When we're done, write my Business Brain in this exact shape, inside one copy box, under 400 words:

BUSINESS BRAIN: [business name]
How to use this: You are my business assistant. Use this in every answer. Write like me. Keep answers short and easy to read on a phone. If something is missing, ask me. Never make up prices, facts, or promises. Everything you write is a draft; I check it before I send it.
1. What we sell:
2. Who buys:
3. Prices and offers:
4. How we talk:
5. What makes us different:
6. Questions customers ask (with answers):
7. Never do:
8. This month's goal:
Last updated: [today's date]

Then tell me in one sentence: "Copy everything in the box and save it in your project (Step 3)."

Start now with question 1.
Tip: tap the microphone and talk your answers. It's faster, and it sounds more like you.

3Save

When your AI finishes, it gives you your Business Brain in a box. Tap the copy button on that box (or press and hold to select all of it, then Copy).

The chat alone doesn't save it. This step does. Pick your app:

Which AI do you use?

Claude

  • Go back to your My Business project page (not the chat).
  • Tap Add instructions, paste your Business Brain, and save.

ChatGPT

  • Go back to your My Business project page (not the chat).
  • Tap the Sources tab → + → Add text. Paste your Business Brain and save.

Gemini

  • Go back to your My Business notebook page (not the chat).
  • Tap Add source → Add text. Paste your Business Brain and save.

Grok

  • Go back to your My Business project page (not the chat).
  • Tap ⋯ → Edit Project. Paste your Business Brain into Project Instructions, then tap ✓.
Done right? Start a new chat in the project and ask "What do you know about my business?" If it answers with your details, it worked.

4Use

Now give your AI real work. Every time:

  1. Open your My Business project.
  2. Start a new chat inside it.
  3. Copy one of the messages below and paste it in.
  4. Replace the words in [brackets] with your details.
  5. Send. Read what it writes, fix anything, and use it.

Reply to a customer

Reply to this customer message. Keep it short and friendly, in my voice: [paste their message]

Follow up with someone who went quiet

Write a short follow-up text to [name], who asked about [what] [when]. No pressure, just helpful.

Plan your day

I have [how much time] today. Based on my goal this month, what are the 3 most important things I should do?

Stay safe

  • Never paste passwords, card numbers, or private customer details.
  • Always read before you send. Your AI writes drafts; you're the boss.
  • In your app's settings, look for the option that lets the company use your chats to train its AI. Turn it off if you want more privacy.

Keep your brain up to date

When something changes (a new price, a new offer, a new goal), open your project and send this:

Update my Business Brain with this change: [what changed]. Give me the full new version in one copy box.

Then paste the new version over the old one in your project. Two minutes, about once a month.
